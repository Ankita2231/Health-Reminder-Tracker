About SDG 3

SDG 3 stands for Sustainable Development Goal 3, which is one of the 17 global goals established by the United Nations in 2015. Its official mandate is to "ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for all at all ages."

Goals of SDG 3:

The goal of Sustainable Development Goal 3 (SDG 3) is simply stated as: "Ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for all at all ages." It addresses all major global health priorities, aiming to tackle infectious diseases, reduce maternal and child mortality, and improve overall physical and mental well-being.

Importance of the SDG 3:

SDG 3 aims to prevent needless suffering from preventable diseases and premature death by focusing on key tragets that boost the health of a country's overall population.
